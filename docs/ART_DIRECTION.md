# Visual systems / anti-drift

The math is not decoration—the transform actually rearranges geometry.

## The visual grammar

- Generators: rings, roses, radial cuts, satellites, flowing threads, spirals, trochoid-like tracing curves, stars, tessellated orbitals.
- Operator: genuine circular inversion with controlled center and radius; poles produce unbounded parts, not mysteriously patched curves.
- Glyph finish: solid black, pure white or transparent ground, broad lines constructed from **filled vector paths**, generous negative spaces and zero raster texture.
- Composition: nine independently selectable compact figures; no intersecting artboards; no labels within exported art.
- Color/animation stage: separate downstream effects through `op_art_style`, `sparklebae`, `color_cycling` etc. This repo's baseline is monochrome.

## Failure modes

- Do not just rotate/reflect shapes and claim inversion occurred.
- Do not add random spiderwebs as proof of mathematical depth.
- Do not allow the image generator to invent false equations.
- Do not flatten SVG export into a photograph and call it vector output.
- Do not silently lose detail by applying hard raster downsampling; provide individual full-size SVGs.
- Do not accidentally connect paths across ideal infinity at the inversion pole.
- Do not merge the personal research library into the commercial SKU.
